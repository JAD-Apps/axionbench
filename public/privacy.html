Privacy

AxionBench is a public, static observatory. Browsing requires no account. The site does not add analytics trackers, advertising cookies or a personal profile.

Your model selections and scenario settings appear in the address bar so you can share a comparison. Anyone with that link can read those settings. Downloads are created in your browser.

The hosting provider receives ordinary web requests, including connection information needed to deliver the site. Original result and pricing links lead to external publishers whose own privacy policies apply.

Fonts and graphics are served with the site. No paid model evaluation or API request runs when you adjust a task-cost scenario.
